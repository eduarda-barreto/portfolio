Core Principles

1

Sensemaking over Reporting

Research succeeds when it restructures the questions a team asks, not just when it answers the ones they had. The deliverable is organizational understanding, not a document.

2

Attitudinal + Behavioral Triangulation

Stated preferences and observed behavior frequently diverge. That gap, between what people say, do, and mean, is where the most consequential insights live.

3

Research as Organizational Memory

Findings compound when they are findable. Knowledge architecture, behavioral taxonomies, and synthesis standards are infrastructure investments that pay returns over time.

4

Upstream Positioning

Research positioned at validation arrives too late to shape the question. The highest-leverage entry point is upstream, at hypothesis formation and opportunity definition.

04 · Research Approach

How I
Think

06
i

Attitudinal & Behavioral Triangulation

Mixed Methods

Stated preferences and observed behavior frequently diverge. I design studies that capture both, because the divergence is where the most consequential insight lives, not an anomaly to be averaged away.

ii

Upstream Research Positioning

Strategic Influence

Research positioned at validation arrives too late to shape the question. I work to embed research influence upstream, at hypothesis formation and opportunity definition, where it has the highest organizational leverage.

iii

Compounding Knowledge Systems

Research Operations

Each study should build on what came before. I invest in the taxonomic and operational structures that make institutional knowledge accumulate, remain findable, and inform future work without requiring rediscovery.

iv

Governed AI Augmentation

Methodological Integrity

AI extends research capacity for synthesis, retrieval, and pattern recognition. I design AI-augmented workflows with explicit epistemic constraints that prevent synthetic signal from substituting for primary behavioral evidence.

v

Cross-Functional Sensemaking

Organizational Influence

Research creates organizational value only when it changes how decisions are made across functions. I design stakeholder rituals and insight communication formats that ensure findings enter product, design, and business conversations.

vi

Behavioral Frameworks as Shared Language

Synthesis & Impact

The highest-leverage research output is a durable mental model, experience principles, decision frameworks, attitudinal segments, that gives teams a shared conceptual vocabulary for customer-centered thinking beyond any single study.
